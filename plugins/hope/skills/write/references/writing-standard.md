# Plain writing standard

Make the meaning easy to see.

## Write directly

1. Lead with the conclusion, decision, or requested result.
2. Prefer a direct sentence and a short, familiar word when they preserve the
   meaning.
3. Remove words that add no meaning.
4. Prefer active voice when naming the actor clarifies the action.
5. Replace stale metaphors, stock phrases, jargon, and unfamiliar borrowed
   terms. Explain a necessary technical term when it first appears.
6. Break any rule when following it would make the writing inaccurate, unclear,
   unnatural, or needlessly harsh.

These principles adapt George Orwell's six rules in
[Politics and the English Language](https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/essays-and-other-works/politics-and-the-english-language/).

## Give the text visible structure

- Let the current purpose, reader, and next action decide what belongs.
- Give each sentence one main idea and each paragraph one clear job. Keep
  related sentences together when separation would harm meaning or voice.
- Use a heading when it helps retrieval or understanding, and a list for a real
  set, choice, sequence, or comparison.
- Use the target format's native paragraph structure. In Markdown and plain
  text, separate paragraphs with one blank line.
- Consolidate repeated framing only when each copy adds no distinct meaning and
  the remaining text still works on its own.
- Use an established semantic structure for a prerequisite, caveat, or next
  action the reader must notice.

## Preserve the work

- Preserve meaning, facts, uncertainty, citations, numbers, and intended voice.
- Do not silently narrow, expand, resolve, delete, demote, or reorder a material
  claim without authority.
- Do not silently resolve a material ambiguity.
- Keep exact code, commands, identifiers, interface text, quotations, and legal
  language unless the person asks to change them.
- Keep a technical term when it is more precise than an everyday substitute.
- Write each language as original prose rather than copying another language's
  word order or idioms.
- Leave typography and styling to the renderer, and treat sentence length as a
  signal rather than a fixed limit.
- Follow a stricter project rule when it serves a clear local need.

## Check once

Before returning the text, confirm that the main point appears early, every
sentence and paragraph has one job, nothing removable remains, and the result
still preserves the original facts, certainty, citations, and voice. Read it
once for naturalness, including translation-shaped phrasing.
