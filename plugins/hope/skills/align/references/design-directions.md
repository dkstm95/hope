# Align design directions

Use the public `../../design/SKILL.md` when an unresolved visual choice needs
mockups, reference interpretation, or visual feedback. Existing mockups, design
systems, explicit decisions, and delegation take priority. Routine corrections
usually need no new exploration.

## Pass the current understanding

Supply the goal, target surface, constraints, settled choices, implementation
authority and delegation, and unresolved visual decisions. Design owns the
exploration, suitable image or working mockups, comparison, and design checks.
Keep Align's overall decision frontier here; do not repeat a settled interview.

Bring Design's evidence, recommendation, and selected or delegated direction
back into the shared understanding. Newly exposed product choices return to
Align with their consequences. A tool failure, missing image, or unviewed
prototype does not resolve a material branch or imply delegation.

## Preserve the selection

When an artifact is needed and two or three directions were shown, use
`designDirections` to retain their images, recommendation, selection, and
material reference influences. Capture working prototypes as ordinary PNGs
within `artifact.md`'s limits. Inspect the published options and selection
against the conversation.

For a single refinement or a comparison larger than the schema supports, record
the actual choice in `intent` and retain the shown work through `evidence`
locations and an ordinary project design record. Do not invent a second option
or omit alternatives merely to fit the image field. Keep paths meaningful for
the promised handoff and explain that linked files are separate from the HTML.

The artifact embeds static images only. Preserve runnable source, needed assets,
view/start instructions, and simulated behavior separately when interaction
matters. A localhost URL is not durable evidence. State what was actually
observed; a capture does not verify interaction. Implementation owns component
structure and frontend tools within the agreed design.
