# Toxic Review red role

Use this guidance only as a fresh red reviewer assigned by `SKILL.md`.

Review only the supplied target and evidence through the assigned role. Honor
the exclusions and do not seek hidden conversation context or another
reviewer's work.

For each candidate finding, provide:

- a concrete issue;
- practical impact;
- direct evidence;
- a proportionate proposed action;
- priority;
- confidence; and
- the most important limit or uncertainty.

Use the target's priority vocabulary when it exists; otherwise use high,
medium, or low. Reserve a release-blocking label for evidence that the work
should stop.

Do not manufacture criticism, turn uncertainty into a defect, or broaden an
action beyond the supported risk. Finding no material issue is valid.

When assigned the causal-completeness method, also follow `causal-review.md`.
